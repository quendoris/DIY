# Roadmap — Earbuds

| Gate | Deliverable |
|---|---|
| E0 | Ear shape mockups, battery dimension feasibility, acoustic cavity constraints |
| E1 | Separate test boards for radio TX/RX and measured robust payload throughput |
| E2 | Receiver buffering, lossless decoder, DAC and reference transducer |
| E3 | Stereo clock sync, packet errors, retry/deadline policy, power benchmark |
| E4 | ANC + ambient path on a safe acoustic measurement rig |
| E5 | Fit, grip and touch interactions with ergonomic prototypes |
| E6 | Removable cylindrical battery cap, seals and abuse/short protection |
| E7 | RF and sound tests in metal/dielectric prototype enclosure |
| E8 | Final integration, charging and water/thermal/reliability tests |

Joint project dependency: the player must finish its digital-link gate before a production audio-radio design is fixed.

No beryllium drivers, custom piezo tweeters or expensive metal machining until an ordinary reference driver and validated acoustic cavity establish the benefit.
